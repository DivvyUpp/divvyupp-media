# Learnings: what works for @getdivvyupp

The rulebook. A rule enters after two supporting Reels and leaves after two contradicting ones (see the noise rules
in `PLAYBOOK.md`). Each rule cites Reel ids from `experiment-log.csv`. Outside ideas live in `backlog.md` until our
own data backs them.

## Confirmed rules
None yet (maturity levels in `PLAYBOOK.md` noise rules).

## Promising signals (2026-11-28; not rules, each from one or two Reels)
- **Empty or static screens lose people.** Both Study Reels with a "tip #N" bridge dropped at 4.5–9 s, exactly on
  that screen; the tier list, with something new every 1.5–3 s and an open S row, had no cliff
  (`research/2026-11-27-retention.md`; 2026-11-26-s10-joint, 2026-11-26-s11-fights, 2026-11-27-savings-tiers).
  Built into the V2 standard; the V2 Reels test it.
- **Couples and savings-rate topics reached 100+** (S10 146 views, 1 share, 1 save; tier list 132), but every Reel
  posted from 2026-11-26 rose at once, so topic and account-level distribution are confounded.
- **Zero follows from ~900 views** (followers 3 since 2026-11-20): nothing yet gives a stranger a reason to come back.
- **Audience skews 18–34 (76–82%)**; our buyer is 35–44 (2.6–9.5%). US nouns (named banks) gave 83% US.

## What the data says so far (2026-11-20)
- **Reach is the bottleneck.** The two Reels with stats got 16 and 21 views (2026-11-14-a, 2026-11-15-a).
- **Most viewers leave in the first 3 seconds.** Skip rates were 90.9% and 85.7% on those two Reels, against a
  small-account average of about 65%. Too few views to judge, but it points at the opening.
- **No Instagram visits to the site yet** on the days those Reels posted (funnel W46).
- All 8 Reels to date share one story template (60–90 s: pain → four numbers → guide → payoff → guarantee). The
  template itself has never been tested against another format.

## Update 2026-11-20
- Reach is still the limit: the 2026-11-16 to 11-19 Reels got 3–12 views each; 3 followers; 2 non-follower views on
  2026-11-19; 0 Instagram landings on 2026-11-19. All inconclusive. 2026-11-15-a has grown to 31 views (skip 90.3%).
- Every Reel so far opens on a blank screen with the presenter fading in; text appears ~0.7s in (execution, not yet
  tested; trial 2026-11-20-t1 tests the fix).
