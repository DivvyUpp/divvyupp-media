| S1 | approved (Srini 2026-11-24) | "Naming your savings goal raised savings 16%. 'Remember to save' reminders did nothing." | Savers got monthly reminders. In Peru, reminders that named the specific expense they were saving for raised savings by an estimated 16% vs no reminder; reminders that didn't name it had no effect.
