# Research brief 2026-11-21 (manual re-run, fresh Shorts pull)

This replaces the earlier 2026-11-21 brief, which had no `YOUTUBE_API_KEY` and reused the 2026-11-20 data.
Backlog numbers below are after today's re-sort.

**Question:** Our first proven-format Reel got people past the opening but not to the end or to share, so which
of our backlog topics hold up on fresh data, and what do the outliers do about length and endings that earns
watch time and sends?

**Bottleneck it serves:** the split-screen "Same $10K a month" Reel (media 18125518024938049, posted 2026-11-20
6:42pm ET) is our first past 100 views: 105 views, reach 89, skip 66.3% (earlier Reels 85–92%), avg watch 16.7 s of
53 s (0.31), 0 shares, 1 like (`metrics/2026-11-21.json`, read at ~16 h, so not the 72 h verdict). The opening got
better. Now the weak signals are **watch-through and sends**. Followers are still 3.

**Source types today:** a fresh mine and kill pass over our own Shorts pull (23 channels), a duration and like-rate
analysis of those same Shorts, platform guidance on sends (secondhand), and Redfin housing data.

**Data:** `research/shorts-2026-11-21.csv`: 837 Shorts from the last 90 days (last 50 uploads per channel) across the
20 niche/fintech channels plus 3 format teachers, using the YouTube Data API. I left out SoFi's celebrity/event clips
(median 841 views, outliers up to 3,370x, almost certainly paid) and sponsored posts ("Presented by", "AD"). Ramsey
Solutions channels count as one org. Everything below is graded **B** (Shorts stand in for Reels) unless noted.

## Findings
1. **New proven topic: parents' money dilemmas (3 orgs at 3x+, 90 days).** Ramsey: Rachel Cruze "Should Parents
   Spend The Same Amount On Every Child's Wedding?" 442,075 views vs her median 46,380 = 9.5x (Oct 16,
   [link](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/so5rD8TaxXw)); George Kamel "4 Accounts Every Parent Should Know" 107,948 vs
   12,715 = 8.5x (Nov 1, [link](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/5JO7M1svezM)). Mrs Dow Jones "What does it actually
   cost to socialize with New York City's most elite moms?" 65,562 vs 2,392 = 27.4x (Oct 15,
   [link](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/jf_8UFYhyos)). Anthony O'Neal "$100 every time you leave the house, and the
   family safety net keeps getting thinner." 11,000 vs 2,716 = 4.1x (Oct 6,
   [link](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/lfj2wimf65A)). Grade **B**. **Applies:** this is our audience (parents with
   kids), and it can ride proven format #1 (two parents disagree). New backlog **#4**.
2. **Proven formats still hold on the fresh pull.** Couple conflict (#1): NerdWallet "Wait… THAT Was Her
   Dealbreaker?!" 98,522 vs 2,026 = **48.6x** (Nov 17, [link](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/CHNkGgF2kks)), Rachel
   Cruze "Her Husband Said He'd Loan Her Money For Groceries" 9.1x, Ramit Sethi 4.9x. Rules list (#3): Rachel Cruze
   7.7x, Kamel "Frugal Hills" 6.8x, Clark Howard 5.4x, NerdWallet 5.0x, and now Graham Stephan "Ranking Money Habits"
   7.8x (4 orgs). Compare (#2): Money Guy 1980 32.5x, Kamel net worth vs a 35-year-old 4.9x, Humphrey 4.1x. Grade
   **B**. **Applies:** Create can keep building in #1–#3 with confidence; #1 has the newest and biggest hit.
3. **Shorter is not what breaks out.** I pulled durations for all 837 Shorts. In 12 of 17 channels with outliers,
   the 3x+ Shorts were as long as or longer than that channel's typical Short (e.g. Rachel Cruze: outliers median
   105 s vs 76 s; Anthony O'Neal: 110 s vs 76 s). Shorts of 35 s or less broke out 9–10% of the time; 61–90 s ones
   13%. Grade **B** (our own analysis; YouTube gives views, not watch time). **Applies:** kills the "30–40 s
   loop" bet as a reach play (moved to #21, the format failed in the data rather than lacking it). It backs Srini's
   clarity-first, up-to-90 s preference. Our 0.31 watch ratio is a pacing problem in the middle, not a length problem.
4. **Breakout Shorts get fewer likes per view.** In 13 of 15 channels with likes data, the outliers' like rate was
   lower than the channel's median (e.g. George Kamel 1.50% vs 2.49%, Mrs Dow Jones 1.27% vs 1.89%). When a post
   reaches strangers, more people watch and fewer of them like it. Grade **B**. **Applies:** on a Reel that breaks
   out, a low likes-per-100 doesn't mean it lost. See proposed change 1.
5. **Ending on a question that splits viewers shows up on 2 big outliers.** Rachel Cruze's wedding Short (9.5x)
   says in its text "I need to know who you agree with"; NerdWallet's new-car red flag (15.0x) asks "Do you agree
   with…?". Only 1 of 837 Shorts asked people to "send this to" someone (Humphrey, 1.9x, a miss). I found no
   published test of a "send this to" CTA on Reels. Grade **B** for the posts, **C** that the question caused the
   reach. **Applies:** new backlog **#16** (needs proof): end on "Who's right?" rather than a statement. Sends per
   reach is still the signal Mosseri names as one of the most important for reaching non-followers (secondhand via
   [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources//instagram-algorithms/) and
   [Uppbeat](https://uppbeat.io/blog/creator-news/whats-working-on-instagram-right-now-carousels-and-sends-says-adam-mosseri),
   grade B). The "DMs count 3–5x likes" figure some guides repeat comes from a vendor, not Instagram, grade C, not used.
6. **House affordability (#5) failed its first direct test, but the numbers to use are solid.** The closest exact
   match in the pull, Humphrey Yang "Who Can Actually Afford a Home In 2027?", got 151,290 views vs 80,793 = **1.9x**
   (Oct 19). No account reached 3x on the topic. Redfin (Aug 5, 2026, June data, 30% rule, 15% down): **$109,796 a
   year** to afford the typical US home vs median household income $87,599; starter home $70,693; San Francisco
   $453,205 ([Redfin](https://www.redfin.com/news/affordability-homebuying-2026/)). Grade **A** for the numbers.
   **Applies:** #5 stays "supported" (format #2 is proven) but drops behind the parent topic. If built, use Redfin's
   assumptions on screen, not ours mixed with theirs.

   lower like rate (finding 4), so don't call it a loss on likes alone.
2. **Script checklist item 5 (length):** today's data supports leaving the 90 s ceiling as it is and not testing a
   shorter cut as a reach lever. When watch-through is low, fix the middle (a rehook at ~40%) before cutting length.
3. **Watchlist:** add parent/household-money creators so the parent topic (#4) and family-spend (#15) kill tests have
   more than one account per org to test against (vet with `scripts/vet_channels.py`).
