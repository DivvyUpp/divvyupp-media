# Research run 2026-11-20 (first scheduled run, 07:00 ET)

The seed brief above was written by hand earlier today; this section is the first daily run. Source types today:
platform guidance and data (the seed brief asked for grade A/B evidence before leaning on Chris Chung's titles).

**Question:** At 3 followers, can @getdivvyupp reach non-followers at all (Trial Reels or otherwise), and which
signals does Instagram use to push a small account's Reel to strangers?

Bottleneck it serves: reach and 3-second hold (`learnings.md`: 3–31 views per Reel, 0 site visits).

## Findings
1. **Trial Reels probably need 200+ followers; we have 3.** Planoly (Oct 23, 2026) quotes Instagram Help: public
   account, eligible for recommendations, and at least 200 followers on a professional account (1,000 on a personal
   one). Metricool's help centre (Nov 2026) gives the same 200 minimum and the API error text: "The instagram account
   does not meet the trial reel follower requirement to create trial reels". Instagram checks this at publish time;
   it cannot be pre-checked. 2025 press said 1,000 ([Social Media Today, Jul 2025](https://socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-expands-access-trial-reels/752895/)).
   Grade **B** (vendors quoting Instagram's help page; I could not open the help page itself).
   Sources: [Planoly](https://www.planoly.com/blog/how-to-post-a-trial-reel-on-instagram),
   [Metricool help](https://help.metricool.com/how-to-publish-instagram-trial-reels-in-metricool-q27tj).
   **Applies:** tonight's approved trials 2026-11-20-t1/-t2 will likely be rejected by Instagram. Backlog bet 1
   (and bet 7, which rides on t1) cannot run until we pass ~200 followers. Metricool also reports a separate,
   undisclosed per-account cap on trial posts through the Content Publishing API.
2. **Instagram's three named Reels signals: watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach.** Adam Mosseri, Jan 2025:
   likes count slightly more with followers, sends slightly more with non-followers. Grade **A** (Instagram head, via
   [Social Media Today, Jan 22, 2025](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/)).
   **Applies:** with 3 followers, almost every view we want is a non-follower view, so **sends** (DM shares) are the
   lever we have never designed for: all 8 Reels have 0 shares. Our audience is two-income couples, which gives a
   natural "send this to your partner" moment.
3. **Recommendations are earned in tiers.** Instagram (Apr 2024) shows eligible original content to a small group
   first and widens it for the best performers; reposts and aggregators are pushed down. Grade **A** (Instagram
   statement, [TechCrunch, Apr 30, 2024](https://techcrunch.com/2024/04/30/instagram-is-updating-its-ranking-systems-to-surface-more-content-from-smaller-original-creators/)).
   Meta's Q3 2026 earnings call (Oct 21, 2026) said nearly 45% of recommended Reels are under a day old. Grade **B**
   (secondhand, via [Keywords Everywhere](https://keywordseverywhere.com/news/instagram-algorithm-updates/)).
   **Applies:** our 3–31 views look like Reels that never pass the first test group. Daily posting fits the
   freshness bias; the lever is the first group's watch time and sends, not hashtags.
4. **Trials help trend-style content, not product pitches (one small test).** Kapwing posted two Reels each as a
   trial and as a regular post: the trend-based one got 2,258 trial vs 949 regular views; the product one got 802 vs
   836, with 0 likes as a trial. Grade **B** (author's own numbers, 2 Reels, no repeats).
   [Kapwing, Oct 2025](https://www.kapwing.com/resources/instagram-trial-reels-study-what-we-learned-after-a-trial-reel-hit-1-million-views/).
   **Applies:** all our Reels are product explainers. Once trials unlock, a format/trend-led variant is more likely to
   gain from them than a re-hooked explainer.
5. **Trending audio beat voiceover by 24% in views at equal watch time (one video, three versions).** Buffer's own
   test; it also reports its trials "topped out at about 180 views" against 1,500–5,000 for regular Reels.
   Grade **B** (one video). [Buffer, Feb 2025](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-trial-reels/).
   **Applies:** weak, and we likely can't attach Instagram library audio through the Windsor API (not verified).
   Noted, no bet today.
