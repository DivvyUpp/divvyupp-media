# Research watchlist

The accounts the Research run uses to find formats that work (**Mine**) and to test our own ideas (**Kill**).
Numbers are from the YouTube Data API on the date checked. Click a name to open the channel.

## Entry bar
**Niche list** (personal and household money). All five must hold:
1. **Topic:** most of its recent Shorts are about personal or household money: budgeting, saving, spending, debt,
   couples or family money, money news for your wallet. Not investing-only, crypto, business-only or comedy.
2. **Active on short-form:** 10+ Shorts in the last 90 days (enough to work out its typical views and find outliers).
3. **Established:** 50,000+ subscribers.
4. **US audience:** US-based or clearly US-focused content.
5. **Original:** the creator's or brand's own videos, not a clip-repost account.

**Fintech brands:** the same bar, for money apps and banks. They show which formats work for a product account like ours.

**Format teachers:** people who test and teach short-form formats in any niche, ideally with their own numbers. They
keep us out of a finance-only silo. Their ideas still have to pass the Kill test before they're ranked.

**Counting toward the 3-account bar:** channels from the same organization count as one (the four Ramsey Solutions
channels are one).
