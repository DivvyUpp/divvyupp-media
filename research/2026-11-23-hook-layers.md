# Research: the four-layer opening (2026-11-23, from Srini's screenshots of @hilzuckerman)

Source: an Instagram Reel by @hilzuckerman (verified creator-growth coach), "Want to make your next reel impossible to
scroll…", seen by Srini 2026-11-23. Engagement at the time: 1,012 likes, 1,217 comments, 235 sends, 8 reposts,
1,443 saves (views not visible). Grade C: one creator's framework, but saves > likes on her own Reel.

## The framework: build the opening in four layers
1. **Get inside someone's head:** one person, her problem, in her words.
   "If you've been posting for weeks and nobody's following you, I want you to try this."
2. **Make the problem worth solving:** add the stakes, what it's costing her.
   "…because you could be making great ideas that reach thousands and still give them no reason to follow."
3. **Make her question what she believes:** a new angle.
   "Your problem might not be how often you're posting. It might be that nobody knows what they'll get if they follow you."
4. **Tell her exactly what she'll get:** a clear promise. "Here are three things I'd fix on your profile today."
Closing card, labelled "screenshot this": before you post, check: one person? her problem? something new? a clear promise?

## Against our Reels
- We usually do layer 1 ("Went in for milk. Came out with $140.") and skip 2–4: no stakes, no reframe up front,
  no promise of what's coming. Consistent with skip rates of 71–92% (metrics/2026-11-22.json).
- Her bigger point, "nobody knows what they'll get if they follow you", applies to the account: @getdivvyupp mixes a
  mascot, couple drama and a founder video with no single promise.
