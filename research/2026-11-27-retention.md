## Findings
1. **The "Here's research-backed money tip #N" bridge is the leak.** In both Study Reels the cliff lands exactly on that
   line (a near-empty screen promising a tip without giving it). It loses ~40% of the viewers the hook earned within
   ~3 s. Rule: no gap between hook and answer; something new on screen at least every 2 s.
2. **A list with an open slot holds.** The tier list has no cliff and kept ~1 in 5 viewers for 45 s (Study Reels: ~1 in
   10 by 15 s). The empty S row is the open loop.
3. **Recaps and late prompts lose people.** The tier list's last drop starts at the recap ("the move"); the send prompt
   at the end reaches the fewest viewers. Put the send/save line right after the payoff, cut the recap.
4. **Concrete US nouns pick the country.** Chase, Wells Fargo, I bonds, $10,000 → 83% US. Couples topics without US
   anchors → 44% US (Australia/Canada next).
5. **None of the three reached our buyer.** 35–44 was 2.6–9.5%; 18–34 was 76–82%. The hook must signal the household
   (mortgage, kids, daycare, two incomes) in the first second. Rates/savings skewed 83% men; couples topics ~58% men.
6. **Posting time looks secondary:** 3:14pm, 7:31pm and 9:59pm all hit the same test-batch ceiling.
