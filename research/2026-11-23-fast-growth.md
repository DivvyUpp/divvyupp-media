# Research: are "100K followers in a few months" claims real, and what do fast growers do? (2026-11-23, asked by Srini)

Grades: A = platform data/statement, B = study with numbers (secondhand), C = self-report or seller claim.

## How true the claims are
- **Typical growth is slow.** A 2026 study of ~2,000 creators found a median Instagram growth of +0.28% a month; accounts
  at 10K–100K typically gained ~100 followers a month. (B) [SocialHipper](https://www.socialhipper.com/blog/follower-growth-benchmarks-2026)
  Median creator account (1K+ followers) is ~27K followers. (B) [Virev](https://virev.ai/blog/average-instagram-followers)
- **Guides put 0 to 100K at 12–24 months**, without published data. (C) [AiGrow](https://aigrow.me/how-to-get-100k-followers/),
  [Virallized](https://www.virallized.com/how-to-get-100k-followers-on-instagram)
- **The fastest stories mostly come from people selling growth programs** ("100K in 60–90 days" for a coaching
  student). (C) [Grow with Alex](https://growwithalex.beehiiv.com/p/the-truth-about-growing-a-massive-instagram-audience).
  The most credible fast faceless example found: 0 to 15K in 30 days, documented by the team. (C)
  [Stan](https://stan.store/blog/creator-strategies-case-study/)
- **Survivorship bias:** the accounts that tried the same playbook and stayed small don't make videos about it. (opinion)
  [Carl King](https://carlkingdom.com/?p=7968)
- **Real fast growth exists but is rare.** Instagram's own example @pov_husband went 0 to 2M in under a year with one
  recurring idea and one real person. (A) [Instagram creators blog](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/helping-creators-of-all-sizes-break-through)
  A wardrobe account went 4K to 190K in a year, ~6 months after getting serious. (C)
  [IndieHackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/how-i-grew-an-instagram-account-from-4000-followers-to-190k-in-a-year-036687e097)

## What fast growers do that we don't yet
1. **One format that works, repeated** as a series with a real person (@pov_husband). (A, one example)
2. **Volume.** 700M+ posts: >10 posts/week ~79% yearly growth vs 19% at 2–3/week; 93.5% of accounts post weekly or
   less. (B, correlational) [Metricool/HypeAuditor](https://metricool.com/press-release-instagram-content-playbook/).
   Buffer (2M posts) shows the same direction. (B) [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-to-post-on-instagram/)
3. **Same video on several platforms** (TikTok, YouTube Shorts), often bringing an audience over. (C)
4. **Borrowed audiences:** Collab posts reach both creators' audiences and can be recommended. (A) [Instagram](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/tips-for-improving-your-reach)
5. **Bought or faked growth** (bought followers, engagement groups, giveaways). Hurts recommendations and
   credibility. Don't.
