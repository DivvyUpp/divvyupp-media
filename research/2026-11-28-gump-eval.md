# Gump evaluation (2026-11-28, chat session; Creator plan, MCP connector)

**Verdict: keep, use it for creative teardowns in Research; don't treat its numbers as baselines or its
"intrigue" curve as retention data.** Cost so far: 0 credits, 0 of 60 Super Searches (all calls below are free tools).

## What it gives (checked)
- A library of 50K+ view TikTok/Instagram videos with Gump ids, searchable by meaning (`search_library`) or live
  (`search_live`). Niche `app-finance-vie-pratique` ("Finance & daily life app"): 792 English videos, median 184K.
- Per video (`get_video_analysis`): why it went viral (one line), format, the hook (said + on-screen + visual) as a
  fill-in template, and beats with timestamps, each with a role, a lever and an **"intrigue" score**.
- Niche stats (`analyze_niche`, `query_library`): share and median views by format, scene, length and hook type.

## Limits (checked)
- **No creator baseline.** Views are absolute; nothing says whether 1.4M is normal for that account. Our 3x-own-median
  test still has to be done separately (YouTube API, or `search_live` on the account).
- **"Intrigue" per beat is Gump's estimate from the video, not a measured retention curve.** Use it as a hypothesis.
- **Winners only** (50K+ views): no ordinary posts to compare against. Niche lifts compare winners with winners.
- Analyses came back **in French** (account language). Set Gump's account language to English.

## First findings worth a bet (each one video: hypotheses, not rules)
1. **@neuronvisuals "INCOME / EXPENSES"**, Instagram, 7.2M views (2026-10-18): faceless, speechless, 12 s. Animated
   container: income trickles in slowly, expenses rush out fast. Hook = the contrast of two motions; no words needed.
   Supports the V2 idea (contrast as a visible state change) and suggests a test we have never run: a **12–15 s
   wordless visual metaphor**. Our renderer can build it (bars/diverge/counters).
2. **@rachelcruze listener-question reel**, Instagram, 1.4M (2026-11-09; her nearby posts in the library sit at
   128–192K, so roughly 7–10x her usual): "This is not a money problem. You have a marriage problem." Indignation at
   financial control in a couple; on-screen quote of the listener's line. Fits Money & Marriage; a drawn-dialogue
   version is possible without inventing a real person (label as an example).
3. **"Every couple should have four accounts"** (TikTok, 519K): one numbered rule, each account revealed in turn
   (an open list like our tier board), no CTA. Overlaps our S10 move ("one shared account for shared bills").
- Niche stats (selection-biased): Demonstration hooks 3.4x the niche median, POV filming 2.6x, 15–30 s 1.15x;
  Promise hooks (43% of videos) 0.85x.
