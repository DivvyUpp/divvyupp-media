## Pain moments needing proof (Srini, 2026-11-23)
Candidate scenes for the opening line. Research tests each against the proof bar and the audience's own words
(`research/pain-language.md`) before Create builds on it; promote proven ones into bets above, drop ones that fail. Evidence for each: [research/2026-11-23.md](research/2026-11-23.md) finding 1.
- Payday to empty: checking the balance days before the next paycheck. **Leaning fail 2026-11-23** (4 direct title matches, all 1.2–2.5x).
- "Where did it all go?": every charge on the statement looks small; the total doesn't.
- Saving "whatever's left" (nothing is).
- The pause at checkout: card in hand, unsure if it's okay. **Leaning fail 2026-11-23** (both matches missed: 0.3x, 0.7x).
- Guilt after a purchase that was probably fine. Needs proof: one near-miss (Ramit 1.6x).
- Earning more and somehow having less. Needs proof: the one exact match missed (1.2x), and comments pile on big salaries (bet #21).
- The money talk: "we're fine" vs "are we, though?" (any couple). **Proven 2026-11-23** through bet #1 (4 orgs at 3x+).
- Feeling behind ("I make good money and it doesn't feel like it"). **Proven 2026-11-23**, new bet #6 (3 orgs at 3x+).

## Off-Reel actions for Srini (in the Instagram app; the loop never posts or messages for him)
These feed the same signals but aren't Reel bets, so they aren't scored.
- **Seed the first test group.** Instagram tests each Reel on a small group first and widens it for the best
  performers. With 3 followers that group is tiny. Sharing each day's Reel to your own Stories and to a few
  parent/friend group chats gives it real first viewers who watch and send.
- **Make the profile worth following.** Bio chosen 2026-11-23 (see PLAYBOOK "Instagram bio"): set it in the app,
  name field "DivvyUpp · by Srini", and pin 3 Reels: what DivvyUpp is, the founder story, and Dollar Bill.
- **Line up a Collab (backlog #19).** Pick one small couples or personal-finance creator and invite them to
  co-post a Reel; Instagram shows a Collab to both audiences.
- **Set up TikTok and YouTube Shorts for DivvyUpp (backlog #11),** so every Reel can go out on all three.
- **Reply to every comment.** Comments aren't one of the three named signals, but a reply gives a stranger a reason to
  come back (opinion, untested).
