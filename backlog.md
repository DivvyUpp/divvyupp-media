2026-11-20, see `runs/research.md`): 3+ unrelated accounts in the last 90 days, each post at 3x+ its own typical views,
or our own data at 100+ views. Bets without that are marked **needs proof**: Research finds the data before Create
builds them.

## Off-Reel actions for Srini (in the Instagram app; the loop never posts or messages for him)
These feed the same signals but aren't Reel bets, so they aren't scored.
- **Seed the first test group.** Instagram tests each Reel on a small group first and widens it for the best
  performers. With 3 followers that group is tiny. Sharing each day's Reel to your own Stories and to a few
  parent/friend group chats gives it real first viewers who watch and send.
- **Make the profile worth following.** One-line bio for the audience (two-income families, "know what's safe to
  spend today"), and pin 3 Reels: what DivvyUpp is, the founder story, and Dollar Bill.
- **Reply to every comment.** Comments aren't one of the three named signals, but a reply gives a stranger a reason to
  come back (opinion, untested).
