## Cold review (subagent, saw only the script and stills)
- Version A (main hook): "a vague question… nothing makes me feel caught. 'Flexible stuff' is app jargon that doesn't
  match the on-screen text." Would not stay past 3s.
- Version B (#1): "aimed at me, the orange line pops… 'Here's proof' is a promise, and $86 vs $219 pays it off
  fast." Would stay, barely. Small mismatch: the proof is about subscriptions, the claim about all spending.
- Both: likely drop-off at the four-numbers card (~20-30s), "a wall of someone else's budget". The soccer-gear moment
  is the most relatable beat and comes late. Would not share.
- B judged clearly stronger. Suggested: lead with the proof itself ("You think it's eighty-six bucks. It's two-nineteen").
