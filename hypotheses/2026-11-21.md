## What we expect to see, plainly
- Views: at 3 followers almost every view is a non-follower one, so views depend on the first test group watching
  and sharing. A realistic good outcome is 100-500 views; under 100 tells us little.
- Skip rate: the opening shows both people and both five-year totals in frame 1, so fewer people should leave in
  the first 3 seconds than on the presenter Reels (44-90%).
- Watch time: the tiles build one by one and the payoff ("What's the difference?") comes in the last 8 seconds, so
  people who stay past 5 s should stay longer.
- Sends: the "Which one are you?" framing is built to be sent to a partner or friend; even 1-2 sends would be a first.
- Site visits: probably none from one Reel at this size; read by day in the funnel export, not by Reel.
