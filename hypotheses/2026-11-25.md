### Final scripts (diagram format, voice speed 1.15, pauses capped at 0.3 s)
**2026-11-25-s4-lock** (Jessica, 40.9 s)
0. [sighs] "I'll just move it back. It's only this once." [curious] People offered a way to lock their savings saved eighty-one percent more.
1. It slides back, and a year later there's nothing to show. The fix isn't willpower. It's how easy the money is to reach.
2. Economists offered people at one bank an account they couldn't touch until they hit their own goal.
3. After a year, people offered it had saved eighty-one percent more than people who weren't.
4. One trial, in the rural Philippines. And only about one in four people offered it signed up.
5. So keep your goal money in a separate account with no debit card attached.
6. [warm] Send this to the person you share savings with, and set it up together.

**2026-11-25-s7-gym** (Eric, 47.3 s)
0. Divide your gym bill by how many times you actually went. In one study, the big monthly plans cost seventy percent more than paying per visit.
1. We all say "I'll go more next month." The fee comes out either way. It's not laziness. In the study, people guessed they'd go more than they did. Here's the ten-second check.
2. Economists followed seven thousand seven hundred fifty-two members of three US gyms, for three years.
3. Members on plans over seventy dollars a month paid, on average, seventy percent more than paying per visit, for the visits they actually made.
4. Add up three months of fees, and divide by your visits in those three months. More than the drop-in price? Check the cancellation fee, then switch.
5. [playful] Send this to your friend with the gym membership. You know the one.

**2026-11-25-s8-minimum** (Jessica, 47.3 s)
0. The smallest number on your card bill might be the most expensive one. It's the minimum payment.
1. Your payment drifts toward it, and the balance barely moves. But in one experiment, hiding that number changed what people paid.
2. UK cardholders were shown a card statement. Some saw a minimum payment. Some didn't.
3. Among people not paying in full, those shown the minimum paid about ninety-nine pounds. Those who never saw it paid about a hundred seventy-five. Almost double.
4. One lab experiment, but paying more each month clears the balance sooner, with less interest. So set autopay to a fixed amount you choose. Never "minimum."
5. [warm] Send this to anyone with autopay on minimum. It takes two minutes to change.
